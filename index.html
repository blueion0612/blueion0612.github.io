Projects

Hip Exoskeleton Gait Assessment

Private 2026 Python
  • Turns a hip exoskeleton into a gait-assessment platform — clinical metrics from the robot's own sensors alone (hip-joint encoders + thigh IMUs, 100 Hz)
  • Estimates three clinical measures in real time: sit-to-stand power age, Gait Profile Score, and enhanced Gait Variability Index
  • Knowledge distillation — motion-capture and reference-IMU teachers train lightweight onboard student models
  • Validated leave-one-subject-out on ~30 adults aged 21–76, indoors and outdoors, across assistance levels — industry-sponsored R&D
AIIMUEncoder

sEMG Gesture Classifier

Public 2026 Python
  • Six-gesture hand recognition from 16-channel surface EMG at 2 kHz — public GREAT dataset (8 subjects, arm-translation protocol)
  • Classical-ML bake-off across Hudgins-4 and rich-14 feature sets, extended with boosting successors and soft voting
  • Reproduces the original paper's protocol as a baseline, then adds LOSO evaluation — KNN ≈ 98% on full data
  • Graduate coursework, Korea University
AIEMG

Markerless 3D Pose from Two Smartphones

Public 2025 — 2026 Python
  • Reconstructs 3D human motion from two ordinary phone cameras — a green torch flash carries the timing, a printed checkerboard the geometry; no sync hardware, no motion-capture suit
  • Six sequential heuristic correction passes replaced by one windowed spatio-temporal bundle adjustment — reprojection, bone-length, acceleration, and joint-limit residuals solved together with an analytic sparse Jacobian
  • 23.1 mm MPJPE / 19.7 mm PA-MPJPE against synthetic ground truth — a scripted motion projected through the real calibrated cameras, so accuracy is measurable without any recorded footage
  • Measured error budget shows calibration scale, not reconstruction, dominates absolute accuracy — undistorting before stereo calibration takes baseline error from 12.1% to 0.14%
AIVision

PPG Fist Classifier

Public 2025 Python
  • Detects hand state (open vs. fist) from 16-channel smartwatch PPG at 25 Hz — no IMU, no camera
  • 224 engineered features (time, gradient, band-power) → gradient boosting, running in real time over UDP
  • Honest evaluation ladder — zero-shot F1 0.46 → 0.73 with 20 s calibration → AUC 0.94 user-dependent (leave-one-session-out)
AIPPG

IMU Gesture Classifier

Public 2025 Python
  • Recognizes 15 wrist gestures in real time from a smartwatch IMU stream (6 channels, 50 Hz over UDP)
  • Two-stage pipeline — an entry detector answers when, a classifier answers which; six architectures grid-searched (MLP, LSTM, GRU, TCN, CNN variants)
  • Best pair in deployment: LSTM entry detection + TCN classification — powers IVO's gesture commands
